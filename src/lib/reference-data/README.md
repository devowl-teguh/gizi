# Clinical reference data

`who-wfa-daily.json` contains L, M, S parameters by day for weight-for-age from the **WHO Child Growth Standards (2006)** expanded z-score tables, separately for girls and boys; age 0–1,856 days, weight in kg. Index equals age in days. The values were extracted without rounding from the first four columns (day, L, M, S), with all 1,857 sequential rows checked for each sex.

- [WHO weight-for-age source page](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards/weight-for-age), expanded tables for national health cards; [girls](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/weight-for-age/expanded-tables/wfa-girls-zscore-expanded-tables.xlsx?sfvrsn=f01bc813_10), [boys](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/weight-for-age/expanded-tables/wfa-boys-zscore-expanded-tables.xlsx?sfvrsn=65cce121_10).
- [WHO dataset terms](https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/data-policy/terms-and-conditions) permit reuse for public health purposes subject to their conditions; WHO does not endorse this product. Confirm terms before other use.
- Implementation calculates age in whole days, applies the LMS formula and the WHO restricted LMS correction outside ±3 SD. Checks compare birth and day 365 values, threshold boundaries, and extreme scores. It is not a clinical validation or a diagnosis. Prematurity, measurement technique, and individual context require professional assessment.
- This covers **weight-for-age only**. Height, weight-for-height, head circumference, and clinical assessment remain out of scope; do not infer nutritional status or overweight from this indicator alone.

## Developmental milestone examples

`src/features/development/milestones.ts` contains four selected observations per stage, paraphrased and translated into Indonesian from the [CDC developmental milestones](https://www.cdc.gov/act-early/milestones/) age-specific pages, accessed 5 November 2026. These are a subset for discussion, not the full checklist or a validated screening instrument; the translation has not undergone professional clinical review. Ages are 2, 4, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 24, 30, 36, 48 and 60 months. CDC says use the younger checklist between stages and use corrected age for a child born more than three weeks prematurely; caregivers are directed to a health professional for corrected-age guidance. For 60–71 months the app uses 5-year examples and advises consultation about age-appropriate assessment. The app never scores the answers or diagnoses development.

## Routine immunization reference

`src/features/vaccines/schedule.ts` reproduces the baby and under-two routine program ages and names in the [Kemenkes 2025 immunization overview](https://kemkes.go.id/id/pekan-imunisasi-dunia-2025-ayo-lengkapi-imunisasi-untuk-generasi-sehat-menuju-indonesia-emas), consulted 5 November 2026. JE is stated as applicable in endemic areas. The source also contains school-age and other recommendations not included in this implementation. This is a static general reference, not a determination of vaccine eligibility, due dates, dose intervals, or catch-up. Names and implementation have not been clinically reviewed. Clinical care and a current official schedule are required for individual decisions; check the source periodically for changes. Dates of actual doses and vaccine brand are not collected, so the tool cannot determine whether a series is complete.

## Length/height-for-age

`who-lhfa-daily.json` contains daily L/M/S values from the WHO Child Growth Standards (2006) expanded tables for [length/height-for-age](https://www.who.int/tools/child-growth-standards/standards/length-height-for-age). Sources: [girls](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/length-height-for-age/expandable-tables/lhfa-girls-zscore-expanded-tables.xlsx?sfvrsn=27f1e2cb_10), [boys](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/child-growth/child-growth-standards/indicators/length-height-for-age/expandable-tables/lhfa-boys-zscore-expanded-tables.xlsx?sfvrsn=7b4a3428_12), accessed 5 November 2026. Each file contains 1,857 day-indexed records (0–1,856), in centimetres. All rows were checked for sequential day indices; tests reproduce selected published values. WHO daily reference switches from recumbent length through day 730 to standing height from day 731. Measurements taken in the opposite position are adjusted by +0.7 cm or −0.7 cm respectively (see [WHO guidance](https://iris.who.int/bitstream/handle/10665/324791/9789241515559-%C2%ADeng.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1)). Age is chronological; premature infants need professional assessment of corrected age. The algorithm uses LMS without the weight-specific tail correction. Translation, implementation and results have not been clinically reviewed and are not a diagnosis.
